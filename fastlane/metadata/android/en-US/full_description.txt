Khmer Calendar is a free, open-source lunar calendar for Cambodia. It shows the Khmer date (lunar day, month and Buddhist Era year) next to the Gregorian date and marks Buddhist holy days and national holidays.

<b>Features</b>
• Month, year, week and day views with Khmer lunar dates
• Holiday and holy day list for any year
• Events and tasks with optional reminders
• Weather for Cambodian cities with an hourly forecast (data from Open-Meteo)
• Material You dynamic colours, light, dark and AMOLED black themes
• Khmer and English interface; choose Saturday, Sunday or Monday as the first day of the week
• Separate notification switches for holy days, public holidays, other festivals and your own reminders
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on your device

<b>Permissions</b>
Internet (weather and city photos), approximate location only when you tap "use my location", and notifications and alarms for reminders.
